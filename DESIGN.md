## Brand & Style

The design system establishes an atmospheric, high-craft, luxury agro-commodity digital experience tailored for fine Chali Cut Supari. It bridges traditional agrarian prestige from the Western Ghats (Sirsi arecanut heritage) with modern luxury e-commerce precision.

Targeted at premium pan shops, institutional merchants, connoisseurs, and retail buyers seeking purity, the visual atmosphere balances deep botanical depth, regal warmth, and tactile refinement. The aesthetic movement merges **Atmospheric Modernism** with **Opulent Tactile Glassmorphism**: deep velvet jade canvases, glowing harvest gold focal points, fine metallic hairline borders, and warm heritage cream neutral surfaces.

### Emotional Resonance
- **Purity & Trust:** Hygienic certification, transparent origin, and unadorned product integrity.
- **Opulence & Craft:** Tactile gold finishes, subtle ambient radiance, and deliberate editorial typography.
- **Direct Prestige:** High-grade agricultural luxury without generic corporate sterility.

## Colors

The color palette centers on the contrast between an impenetrable, deep velvet emerald background and luminous harvest golds, relieved by antique warm cream neutrals.

### Roles & Harmonies
- **Primary (`#E5A93C`):** The signature harvest gold. Used for key call-to-actions, active highlights, badges, and illuminated headings. Supporting tones include glowing champagne gold (`#F4C45E`) and deep aged bronze (`#C69224`).
- **Secondary (`#062319`):** The foundational night jade / deep emerald. Anchors the page canvases, headers, cards, and dark theme sections. Supported by mid-layer forest velvet (`#0B3324`) and jade sheen (`#10402E`).
- **Tertiary (`#25D366`):** Direct contact and conversion accent dedicated to instant WhatsApp ordering and active status indicators.
- **Neutral (`#FBF8EE`):** Antique royal cream. Acts as the high-contrast light section canvas, text on dark emerald backgrounds, and pill badge backgrounds. Supported by muted stone cream (`#F4EEDD`) and subtle parchment border lines (`rgba(251, 248, 238, 0.12)`).

### Contrast Rules
- Harvest gold text (`#E5A93C` / `#F4C45E`) is restricted to display headers and large label chips over `#062319` or `#0B3324`.
- Body copy on dark surfaces must use warm cream (`#FBF8EE` at 90% opacity or `#E1E8E3`) to maintain WCAG AAA compliance.
- Interactive gold buttons use deep obsidian/emerald text (`#062319`) for crisp legibility.

## Typography

The type system pairs an evocative, high-elegance editorial serif (**EB Garamond**) with the structural geometric clarity of **Plus Jakarta Sans** and the technical precision of **Space Grotesk** for product specifications and logistics badges.

### Hierarchy Guidelines
- **Headlines & Narrative Moments:** Rendered in `EB Garamond`. Display weights bring forward artisanal dignity, natural cut textures, and heritage sourcing.
- **Body & Product Descriptions:** Rendered in `Plus Jakarta Sans` for clean, unhindered readability on high-density data surfaces.
- **Eyebrows, Badges, Metrics & Microcopy:** Rendered in uppercase `Space Grotesk` with expanded tracking (`0.08em` to `0.12em`) to provide an authentic certification and commodity trading feel.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy uses a disciplined **fluid grid** anchored by a max content container of `1280px` for hero and transaction workflows, expanding to `1440px` for immersive editorial sections.

### Grid & Breakpoints
- **Desktop (≥ 1024px):** 12-column layout with `gutter-desktop` (2.5rem / 40px) and `margin-desktop` (4rem / 64px). Allows side-by-side packaging showcases and streamlined checkout flows.
- **Tablet (768px – 1023px):** 8-column layout with 2rem gutters and 2rem side margins.
- **Mobile (≤ 767px):** 4-column fluid layout with `gutter` (1.5rem / 24px) and `margin` (1.25rem / 20px). Actions collapse to full-width or sticky bottom interaction drawers.

### Spacing Scale
Component internals adhere strictly to an 8-point rhythmic cadence (`0.25rem` to `4rem`). Dense transaction panels utilize `space-md` (16px) for item rows, while hero sections leverage `space-2xl` (64px) for expansive visual presence.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is achieved through **tonal jade stacking**, **frosted glass refraction**, and **ambient gold luminescence**, avoiding standard gray drop shadows.

### Surface Tiers
1. **Canvas Level (Elevation 0):** Pure dark jade (`#062319`) or warm cream parchment (`#FBF8EE`).
2. **Elevated Containers (Elevation 1):** Midnight jade surfaces (`#0B3324`) overlayed with subtle glassmorphic backdrop filters (`backdrop-blur(16px)`) and a delicate frosted edge border (`1px solid rgba(251, 248, 238, 0.08)`).
3. **Floating Overlay Cards & Modals (Elevation 2):** Layered green slate (`#10402E`) finished with soft directional gold ambient lighting (`box-shadow: 0 20px 40px -15px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.6), 0 0 24px -4px rgba(229, 169, 60, 0.12)`).
4. **Active Controls & Gold Badges (Elevation 3):** High-radiance metallic glows (`box-shadow: 0 8px 24px -2px rgba(229, 169, 60, 0.35)`).

## Shapes

The design system incorporates a **pill-shaped (level 3)** curvature scheme. High roundedness tempers the solemnity of deep emerald green, imparting an approachable, premium botanical confectionery and hospitality impression.

### Curvature Values
- **Base Components (Inputs, Chips, Small Badges):** Full pill (`rounded-full` or `9999px`) to maintain tactile fluid touchpoints.
- **Surface Panels & Hero Cards:** `2rem` (`rounded-lg`) to `3rem` (`rounded-xl`), creating soft organic packaging frames.
- **Thumbnails & Media Containers:** `1.25rem` to `1.5rem` with internal border masks.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary (Harvest Gold CTA):** Filled with solid `#E5A93C` or linear gradient (`#F4C45E` to `#E5A93C`). Text in deep `#062319` with `label-lg` uppercase tracking. Full pill border radius. Subtle gold spread glow on hover.
- **Secondary (Forest Glass):** Background `rgba(11, 51, 36, 0.7)` with `1px solid rgba(229, 169, 60, 0.35)`. Text in `#FBF8EE`. On hover, border transitions to solid `#F4C45E` with warm inner sheen.
- **WhatsApp Direct Action:** Dedicated emerald green `#25D366` pill button with white text and gold ring focus indicators.

### Chips & Badges
- **Status Badges (FSSAI, Bengaluru Only):** Compact pill containers with translucent forest fills (`rgba(6, 35, 25, 0.8)`), fine gold hairline borders, and tiny gold or green icons.
- **Weight Selectors (e.g., 500g Pouch):** Pill-shaped toggle tiles. Unselected state uses muted dark green with subtle border; selected state features an active gold border, `#0B3324` fill, and harvest gold checkmark badge.

### Input Fields
- Dark velvet jade background (`#07281D`), filled with cream text (`#FBF8EE`).
- Hairline stroke in `rgba(251, 248, 238, 0.15)`, transitioning to `#E5A93C` with a soft ambient aura on `:focus-visible`.
- Generous internal padding (`0.875rem 1.5rem`) with pill-rounded outer boundaries.

### Cards & Packaging Showcases
- **Product Showcase Card:** Encased within a large rounded container (`2rem` radius) utilizing a backdrop gradient from `#0B3324` to `#062319`. Features an integrated halo backlight that spotlights the golden D GOLD pouch art.
- **Verification Badges / Floaters:** Translucent floating capsules positioned over imagery with backdrop blur (`12px`) and subtle gold drop shadows.

### Lists & Key Metric Displays
- Feature lists highlight arecanut authenticity with custom gold-accented check icons inside mini circular jade housings.
- Clean separation with parchment-opacity dividers (`rgba(251, 248, 238, 0.08)`).
